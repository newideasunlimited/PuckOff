# Contributing to PuckOff

Thanks for helping.

1. **Write your own code.** Don't copy code from other controller projects
   (several are under licenses incompatible with Apache-2.0). Protocol facts are
   fine; document them in your own words in `docs/protocol.md`.
2. **No vendor binaries or keys** in this repository.
3. **Test on real hardware** when you change firmware behavior, and say in the PR
   which board and host(s) you tested.
4. **Sign off your commits** (`git commit -s`) to certify the
   [Developer Certificate of Origin](https://developercertificate.org/).
   Contributions are licensed under Apache-2.0.

Keep PRs small: one feature or fix per PR.
